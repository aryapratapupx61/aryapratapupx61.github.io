Inside the machine

GODXSHADOW is a browser-side typing test. No backend, no account, no tracking — just keystrokes, math and neon.

the four numbers

  • WPM (net) — correct characters ÷ 5, divided by minutes. One "word" is standardised as five characters, spaces included.
  • Raw WPM — every character you produced, correct or not. It shows your ceiling; the gap to net WPM is what mistakes cost you.
  • Accuracy — correct keystrokes ÷ total keystrokes. Extra characters typed past the end of a word count as errors.
  • Consistency — 100 × (1 − CV) across your per-second WPM samples. 100 = perfectly even pace, low 70s = spiky.

shortcuts

  • any key — start the timer and focus the test
  • space — submit the current word
  • backspace — delete one character
  • ctrl + backspace — wipe the whole word
  • tab — restart the same text
  • shift + tab — restart with a new text
  • esc — abort the current run

scoring rules, precisely

So the number means the same thing every run.

faq

  • Is anything uploaded? No. Runs, personal bests and your callsign live in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data wipes them.
  • Why is my raw WPM higher than WPM? Raw counts everything you typed. The difference is your error tax — closing that gap raises net speed faster than typing harder.
  • What is a good score? Roughly 40 WPM casual, 60–80 professional, 100+ competitive. Accuracy matters more than the peak: 95%+ at 70 beats 82% at 95.
  • Does mobile work? The test runs on any keyboard. Touch keyboards send characters normally, so timing stays fair, though the virtual keyboard preview is desktop-oriented.
  • Can I change the word list? Yes — six banks (common 200, common 500, quotes, code words, numbers, easy) plus hard mixes, punctuation, caps and number injection.
  • What is Page Type? The Passages page. Instead of a generated word stream you type a whole paragraph: warm-ups, home-row ladders, rhythm drills, punctuation and caps, numbers, code snippets, technical prose, short stories and long endurance runs.
  • Can I type my own text? Yes. On the Passages page, write or paste anything into enter your passage, give it a title, then hit type this now or save passage. Saved passages live in this browser, and you can export or import them as JSON.
  • How are passage runs scored? Exactly like timed runs — WPM, raw, accuracy and consistency — except there is no countdown. The clock counts up and the run ends on the final word. Passage results are stored with mode passage and show up in your history.
