how scoring works

  • WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Errors are not free.
  • Raw WPM = everything you typed, right or wrong. The gap between raw and net is your accuracy tax.
  • Accuracy = correct keystrokes ÷ all keystrokes, extras included.
  • Consistency = how flat your per-second pace is. 100 means metronome.
  • Word counts as typed the moment you hit space.

why godxshadow

Built for people who actually watch the numbers.

01 · live telemetry

Numbers while you type

WPM, raw speed, accuracy and error count update every keystroke, with a per-second curve drawn behind the text.

02 · six text banks

Words to quotes to code

Common 200, common 500, quote sentences, programming vocabulary, pure numbers and an easy bank — plus hard mixes, punctuation and caps.

03 · zero upload

Local only

Results and personal bests stay in your browser storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.

04 · page type

Whole passages

Type a full paragraph instead of a word stream — warm-ups, rhythm drills, code, technical prose, stories and long endurance runs.

05 · your own text

Enter your passage

Write or paste anything — an article, your notes, a chapter — save it locally and practise it as many times as you like.

06 · no timer

Finish the page

Passage runs are untimed. The clock counts up, the run ends when the last word does, and the score is yours to keep.
